Privacy

Effective 1 November 2026

Bezzle does not collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it sends nothing about you or your designs to us or to third parties.

Your designs

Designs are files on your Mac, in the Bezzle folder in your Documents or wherever you keep them. Pictures you place are copied into the design. Remove Background runs on your Mac; photos are never uploaded.

Sharing over iCloud

When you share a design, it is stored with Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) in your account and in the accounts of the people you invite. We cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

AI assistants

Bezzle includes a server for AI assistants that listens only on your Mac (127.0.0.1) and cannot be reached from the network. Nothing is shared with an assistant unless you connect one. If you do, what it reads from your design is handled by that assistant and its provider under their own privacy policy.

Network

Bezzle connects to the internet only for these: iCloud, for designs you share; while others have a shared design open with you, a relay run on Cloudflare that passes on your pointers and changes, encrypted end to end with a key only the people in the share have, so that neither we nor Cloudflare can read them; and a web page you choose to bring in, which is loaded as your browser would load it.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS's settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports on to us. You can change this at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Bezzle collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome: get in touch.
